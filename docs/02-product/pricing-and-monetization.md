# Pricing and monetization

## Tiers
| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5 fields, matrix view, sort, text filter, CSV export |
| Pro | $25/yr or $49 lifetime (A/B on landing page) | Unlimited fields, typed/numeric, per-column filters, saved views, XLSX/DOCX export, templates, priority support |
| AI credits (v2) | $8 per 50 credits or $10/mo for 120 | "Fill column from PDF", batch extraction, summaries |
| Lab/Team (v3) | $99/yr per 5 seats | Shared schema in group libraries |

## Why these numbers
- Students said $20–30/yr out loud (Zotero forum, Aug 2026).
- PapersGPT: $7/mo or $39 lifetime; Beaver: $10–20/mo credits. We sit below.
- Elicit charges $49/mo for a 20-column table; we are the "inside Zotero, 1/20th price" option.

## Payment/licensing
- Lemon Squeezy (handles VAT/EU taxes) → license key → offline signed check in plugin.
- 14-day Pro trial on install; no card required.
- Educational goodwill: free Pro for plugin contributors and for librarians who teach it.
