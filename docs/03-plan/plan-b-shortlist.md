# Plan B shortlist (use only if the kill rule triggers)

Kill/pivot rule: forum posts visible from ~Sep 15. If by **2026-09-24** there are fewer
than 5 real signals (forum replies, installs, named testers, emails), stop LitMatrix
polish and pick one of these. Decision should take one hour, using the research in
`docs/01-research/other-pain-points-considered.md`.

## B1 — "Proof I wrote this" kit (AI-accusation defense)
- Pain: detectors false-flag 43–83% of real student writing; students record hour-long
  videos of themselves typing; 6 lawsuits 2024–26; institutional appeal processes now common.
- Product: browser extension that logs typing vs paste with timestamps, hashes it, and
  exports a one-click appeal packet (timeline + Google Docs/Word revision export).
- Why it might beat LitMatrix: larger market (every essay-writing student), strong
  emotion, only one real competitor (Grammarly Authorship, Docs/Word-only, distrusted).
- Why the owner passed: discomfort with the topic. Revisit only if that changed.
- MVP: 2–3 weeks. Price $3–5/mo or $15/semester; B2B to writing centers.
- Validation channel: student subreddits (r/college), TikTok/YouTube "falsely accused of AI" comments, university student-advocate offices.

## B2 — Deadline planner with a real wedge
- Pain: LMS deadline noise, multiple time zones, soft deadlines (online master's);
  ADHD time blindness; syllabus PDFs never make it into calendars.
- Existing: 9+ apps at $5–10/mo (Coursicle, Shovel, Due Gooder, UpAhead, Wick…).
- Only viable with a wedge, pick ONE: (a) grade-weight-aware "what actually matters
  this week" for online/part-time master's programs (OMSCS-style), or (b) SMS-first
  ADHD reminders with body-doubling sessions.
- MVP: 3–4 weeks (Canvas ICS + syllabus PDF parse + weighting). Price $6–9/mo.
- Validation channel: OMSCS/online-master's Slack/Discord communities, r/ADHD college threads.

## B3 — Stay on LitMatrix but change the wedge
- If replies say "nice, but I want AI to fill the columns": lead with AI extraction
  (BYO key free, hosted credits paid) instead of manual fields. Same codebase.
- If replies say "I use Obsidian/Notion for this": build the Zotero→table sync as an
  export target (Notion database / Obsidian Dataview) instead of an in-Zotero UI.

## Not worth revisiting
Quizlet/Anki clones (Knowt owns free; thin moat), group-project tracker (no payer),
past-paper solution generator (integrity risk), subscription-cancel helpers (Rocket Money).
