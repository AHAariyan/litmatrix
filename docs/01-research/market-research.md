# Market research (as of 2026-09-12)

Note: reddit.com was unreachable from the research environment. Evidence below is
from Hacker News (Algolia API), Zotero forums, GitHub, Trustpilot/App Store,
The Student Room (UK), Eurostat/NCES/NSF and 2025–26 press.

## 1. The pain, in users' words
- Zotero forum, Jul 2020 (PhD student): "I am doing a literature review and trying
  to create a review matrix, i.e. a csv file with publication meta data... make my
  phd life much easier." Answer: export CSV, delete columns in Excel.
- Zotero forum, Dec 2022: "Is there a way to compare reference sources in Zotero
  through a table or matrix... methodology, sample size...?" A Zotero team member
  (hsiangyu_wong, Better Notes author) said a Dataview-like "column-customizable
  table" was planned. Not shipped as of Better Notes v3.3.3 (2026-08-24).
- Zotero forum, Sep 2019 → Nov 2023 (7 users): "Request Custom Fields". Staff
  (dstillman): "Custom fields are generally planned, but they present a lot of
  technical challenges... and likely won't happen anytime soon."
- Zotero forum, Apr 23 2026: "the bottleneck is no longer generating useful
  metadata from papers. It is storing and managing that metadata in a first-class way."
- Zotero forum, Aug 2026 (grad student): 6 GB storage at "$60 USD per year...
  prohibitively expensive... at roughly 20–30 USD per year, many budget-constrained
  students like myself would be willing to subscribe."
- Zotero forum, Mar 2026: "Are there student discounts?" Staff: "No."
- Zotero forum, May 2025: unlimited storage is "around 7% of my salary as a researcher".
- HN "Zotero 8" (Jan 2026, 245 pts): "didn't realize sync was paid... at 99.6% of
  the free tier"; "unbearably slow"; upgrade "completely broke" linked files.
- HN "Goodreads for academic papers... help my wife survive her PhD" (Jan 2026):
  commenter: Zotero's to-read workflow "feels overly commitment-heavy"; asked for
  export and search.

| UK PhD students | ~105,000 | UK doctoral education study |
| Researchers worldwide | >2.2M (blog estimate) | 360researchreports |

Zotero publishes no user count. Ecosystem proxies (GitHub stars, 2026-09):
Better BibTeX 7.1k; Better Notes ~7k; Notero (Zotero→Notion) 3.2k;
Zotero Reading List (one custom column) 573; Ze-Notes (table of tagged notes) 106.

Serviceable market estimate: 100–300k Zotero-using grad students/ECRs doing
reviews in target countries. Paid conversion for a $25/yr plugin: 1–3%.
